Custom CNC Machine

A custom-built 3-axis CNC machine based on the PrintNC community platform, modified and refined to suit precision prototyping, small-batch manufacturing, and experimental development work.

Concept & Engineering Intent

This machine was built as both a demonstration of my mechanical and electrical engineering capabilities and as a practical tool to support in-house design, prototyping, and production. While the foundation follows the PrintNC open-source platform, multiple design decisions, modifications, and future upgrades are driven by my own requirements and experimentation.

Machine Capabilities

Configuration: 3-Axis Gantry CNC
Based on: PrintNC Open-Source Platform
Materials Machined: Wood, Plastics, Aluminium, Mild Steel
Frame: Box Steel construction with reinforced gantry
Motion System: Linear rails & ballscrews
Intended Use: Prototyping, tooling, custom components
